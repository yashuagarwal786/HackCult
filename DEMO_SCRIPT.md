# HeritageAtlas — Video Walkthrough Demo Script (2–3 Minutes)
**Track 2: Culture & Digital Heritage (Blithchron '27, IIT Gandhinagar)**  
**Problem Statement ID:** HC26-CULT01  

---

## ⏱️ Video Demo Outline (Total Duration: 2 mins 45 secs)

### 0:00 – 0:30 | Hook & Problem Definition
- **Visual:** Screen shows the HeritageAtlas dashboard with the problem badge "Blithchron '27 · HC26-CULT01".
- **Speaker:** 
  > *"Hello judges! At IIT Gandhinagar, the Explorer Fellowship produces thousands of incredible grassroots cultural photos, manuscripts, and notes. Yet, this rich heritage typically ends up in an 'archival graveyard' — buried in static slide decks and unindexed folders.*
  > *Today, we introduce **HeritageAtlas** — a platform that breathes life into unstructured heritage data across all three hackathon innovation pathways: WebGL 3D digital twins, epigraphic OCR, and geospatial expedition mapping."*

---

### 0:30 – 1:10 | Pathway 1: 3D Digital Twin Studio (WebGL & Three.js)
- **Action:** Click on the **"3D Digital Twin"** tab in the sidebar.
- **Visual:** The 3D Subterranean Stepwell (Rani ki Vav) appears with golden sandstone lighting and reflective water.
- **Action:** Drag to orbit the model 360 degrees. Toggle **"Mesh Wireframe"** to show polygon density.
- **Action:** Click **"Equinox Solar Ray"** to demonstrate the solar beam piercing down to the stepped pool.
- **Action:** Switch the dropdown to **"Konark Chariot Wheel"** and show the radial sundial spokes.
- **Speaker:**
  > *"First, our Computer Vision Pathway: HeritageAtlas converts crowdsourced 2D photos into interactive WebGL 3D digital twins. Here is Rani ki Vav — users can inspect its seven subterranean tiers, toggle wireframe mode to audit polygon geometry, switch between noon and torchlight lighting, and even visualize the equinoctial solar alignment beam that illuminates the sanctum."*

---

### 1:10 – 1:45 | Pathway 2: Vision Transformer OCR & Epigraphy
- **Action:** Click on the **"OCR & Epigraphy"** tab in the sidebar.
- **Visual:** Select the "Devanagari Epigraphic Palm Leaf (Kalinga Inscription)" plate.
- **Action:** Scroll through the side-by-side view (Raw preserved text vs. Normalized transcript + English translation).
- **Action:** In the Correction Ledger below, click the green checkmark to accept a correction.
- **Action:** Click **"Send to Ingestion"**.
- **Speaker:**
  > *"Second, our Archival NLP Pathway: Historical Devanagari Sanskrit, regional Gujarati, and English field notes are processed with our Vision Transformer OCR pipeline. We preserve the raw uncorrected text for epigraphic auditability while generating an English academic translation and an automated architectural glossary. Digital researchers can verify corrections directly in the ledger and send the validated text straight into the ingestion pipeline with one click."*

---

### 1:45 – 2:15 | Pathway 3: Geospatial Explorer & Fellowship Routes
- **Action:** Click on the **"Fellowship Routes"** tab in the sidebar.
- **Visual:** An interactive Leaflet map appears showing India with glowing expedition lines.
- **Action:** Click on the "IITGN Explorer Fellowship: Western Hydraulic & Solar Heritage" trail.
- **Action:** Click on Waypoint **"Day 4: Modhera Sun Temple & Surya Kund"**. The slide-out card opens with field logs, student names, and local folklore.
- **Speaker:**
  > *"Third, our Geospatial Pathway: Instead of flat reports, travel routes are dynamically plotted. Here is the IITGN Explorer Fellowship cohort expedition: from the Gandhinagar campus to Adalaj, Modhera, and Rani ki Vav. Clicking any stop reveals student fellow logs, ambient measurements, and oral folklore traditions associated with that sacred site."*

---

### 2:15 – 2:45 | Dataset Workspace, Fact-Grounded Assistant & Conclusion
- **Action:** Click on **"Monuments Atlas"**, type in *"सूर्य मंदिर"* in the search bar. Show instant filtered results.
- **Action:** In the right drawer, ask the Fact-Grounded Assistant: *"Which monuments use granite?"* The assistant answers with cited record IDs (`HA-0003`, `HA-0005`).
- **Action:** Click **"Dataset Workspace"** and click **"Export GeoJSON (GIS)"** to show the download trigger.
- **Speaker:**
  > *"Finally, our platform features multilingual search in English and Devanagari, a strictly fact-grounded archival assistant that cites record IDs without hallucination, and one-click GeoJSON export for academic GIS software.*
  > *HeritageAtlas bridges grassroots exploration and modern digital revitalisation. Thank you!"*
